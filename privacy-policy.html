Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

Buyolex ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use our website, how we use it and the choices you have. By using the site you agree to this policy.

2. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the shop:

  • Your name, phone number and email address when you create an account, place an order or contact us.
  • Delivery addresses you enter or save in your address book.
  • Your order history and the messages you send us.
  • Basic technical information such as browser type and the pages you visit, to keep the site working well.

3. How we use your information

  • To process, deliver and support your orders, including calling you to confirm delivery.
  • To manage your account and answer your questions.
  • To keep the site secure and prevent fraud or misuse.
  • To send offers or news only if you have subscribed. You can unsubscribe at any time.

4. Who we share it with

We share information only where it is needed to serve you: delivery and courier partners (name, phone, address), mobile payment providers such as bKash or Nagad when you choose them, and authorities where the law requires it. We do not sell your personal information.

5. Cookies and local storage

The site stores small pieces of data in your browser, for example your cart, wishlist, theme choice and login state, so that the shop works as you expect. You can clear this data from your browser settings, but your cart and login will then be reset.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No online service can promise perfect security, so please keep your password private and contact us if you notice anything unusual on your account.

7. Your choices

You can view and update your name, email and WhatsApp number from your profile, and manage your saved addresses from the address book. To correct or delete other information, or to close your account, contact us using the details below.

8. Children's privacy

Our shop is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
