1. Overview

We want you to be happy with every Buyolex piece. If something is not right, this policy explains how returns, exchanges and refunds work. By placing an order you agree to it.

2. When you can return

You can ask for a return or exchange if:

  • the item arrived damaged or defective,
  • we delivered the wrong item or variant, or
  • the item is very different from its description on the website.

Please contact us within 3 days of receiving your order. Requests after that may not be accepted.

3. Conditions

  • The item should be unused and in the condition you received it.
  • Keep the original packaging, and any accessories or papers that came with it.
  • Have your order number ready as proof of purchase.

4. What cannot be returned

  • Personalised or custom-made pieces (for example engraved items from Buyolex Lab), unless they arrive damaged or wrong.
  • Opened or used food and organic products, for hygiene and safety, unless they arrive damaged or wrong.
  • Items that were used, damaged after delivery, or returned without their packaging.

5. How to request a return

  1. Contact us through the contact page, WhatsApp or phone.
  2. Send your order number, the item(s) and the reason.
  3. For damaged, defective or wrong items, attach clear photos.
  4. We review your request and reply within 2 business days. If it is approved, we will guide you through the next step.

6. Exchanges

We can exchange items that arrived damaged, defective or in the wrong variant, subject to stock. If a replacement is not available, we will offer a refund instead.

7. Refunds

Once your return is received and checked, we will tell you the result. Approved refunds go back to the payment method you used. For cash on delivery orders we refund through bKash, Nagad or bank transfer. Please allow 5 to 7 business days after approval; your mobile banking provider or bank may need extra time.

8. Damaged in transit

If your parcel arrives damaged, please take photos of the item and packaging and contact us right away so we can fix it quickly.
